Questions
Good to know.

      

        
Where do you work?
Across Greater Cincinnati. Send your ZIP code with your quote and I'll confirm.

        
Do I need to be home?
Not always. For mobile jobs I need the car reachable, unlocked or with keys handed off, and a flat spot to work.

        
Is there a trip charge?
No. The price on the menu is the price, whether I come to you or you drop off.

        
What if the car needs more work?
I'll show you what I found and quote it. Nothing extra gets done without your OK.

        
Can I bring my own parts?
Ask first. I'll install them if they're the right fit and quality.

        
I live in an apartment. Can you still come?
Yes for quick jobs like batteries, wipers, bulbs and scans. For oil changes, check with your leasing office, book at your workplace lot, or drop off.

        
Which cars do you work on?
Most cars and small SUVs. Trucks, large SUVs and anything unusual get quoted by text.
